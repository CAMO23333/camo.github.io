Motivation

Real motion helps; turbulence pseudo-motion corrupts.

In open-air infrared videos, observed displacement mixes coherent object or camera motion with random refractive perturbations. Treating both as a single motion signal propagates distortion and destabilizes temporal reconstruction.

  1. 01

    Physics-grounded factorization. LMTD uses thermo-radiative motion coherence to separate mixed temporal dynamics into object-motion and turbulence components.

  2. 02

    Role-specific restoration. TCLR suppresses local turbulence perturbations, while MCTE exploits coherent motion for temporal detail enhancement.

  3. 03

    Reliability-guided generation. Motion reliability regulates one-step restoration to preserve constructive cues without amplifying uncertain pseudo-motion.
